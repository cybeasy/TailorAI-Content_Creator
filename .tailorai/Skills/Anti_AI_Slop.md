## 2. Structural Antidotes

### Antidote 1: Cut the Throat-Clearing
- **Bad (AI):** *"In the rapidly evolving landscape of modern marketing, understanding content strategy has never been more crucial for organizations looking to scale."*
- **Good (Human):** *"Most marketing teams spend $20,000 a month publishing articles no one ever reads. Here is why your content strategy is failing."*

### Antidote 2: Vary Sentence Length (The Gary Provost Rule)
> *"This sentence has five words. Here are five more words. Five-word sentences are okay. No one complains about them. But several together become monotonous. Listen to what is happening. The writing is getting boring. The sound of it drones. It’s like a stuck record. The ear demands some variety.*
> *Now listen. I vary the sentence length, and I create music. Music. The writing sings. It has a pleasant rhythm, a lilt, a harmony. I use short sentences. And I use sentences of medium length. And sometimes, when I am certain the reader is rested, I will engage him with a sentence of considerable length, a sentence that burns with energy and builds with all the impetus of a crescendo, the roll of the drums, the crash of the cymbals–sounds that say listen to this, it is important."*

### Antidote 3: Ground in Concrete Specifics
- Replace vague claims (*"we deliver rapid results"*) with exact numbers (*"deployed in under 14 minutes with zero server restarts"*).
