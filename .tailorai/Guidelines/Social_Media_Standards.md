# Social_Media_Standards.md — Multi-Platform Publishing Playbook

> **Purpose:** Structural and formatting rules for short-form and social content across LinkedIn, X/Twitter, Instagram, and Newsletters.

---

## 1. LinkedIn Post Blueprint

- **Word Count:** 150 – 300 words.
- **The 2-Line Hook Rule:**
  - The first two lines decide whether the reader clicks "see more".
  - Never start with pleasantries ("Hope everyone is having a great Monday").
  - Start with a bold counter-intuitive statement, a personal milestone, or a costly mistake.
- **Formatting & Spacing:**
  - Maximum 1 to 2 sentences per paragraph.
  - Generous white space for mobile screens.
  - Use bullet points (`•` or `-`) for lists.
- **The Closing:**
  - Ask an open-ended, debate-provoking question to drive comments.
  - Place links in the designated place (body or first comment based on client rule).

---

## 2. X / Twitter Thread Blueprint

- **Thread Length:** 5 to 8 tweets.
- **Tweet 1 (The Anchor Hook):**
  - High emotional or analytical hook.
  - Explicit payoff statement (e.g., *"Here's the exact framework we used to... 🧵"*).
  - Bookmark incentive.
- **Tweets 2–(N-1) (The Value Nuggets):**
  - One standalone valuable idea per tweet.
  - Can be screenshotted or quoted independently.
- **Final Tweet (The Synthesis & CTA):**
  - Summary takeaway.
  - Repost request ("If you found this valuable, repost Tweet 1").
  - Link to the long-form article or {{PRIMARY_CTA}}.

---

## 3. Email Newsletter Blueprint

- **Subject Line:** Under 50 characters, high curiosity or concrete utility.
- **Preview Text:** 35–45 characters extending the hook.
- **Opening:** Personal, conversational 1-on-1 greeting.
- **Core Story / Lesson:** 400–600 words with one central takeaway.
- **Single CTA:** One focused button or text link to maximize click-through rate.
