# Brand_Voice_Audit.md — Pre-Flight Editorial Audit

> **Audit Checklist:** Run this quick verification pass before submitting any article or post.

---

## 1. Voice Alignment Check
- [ ] Does the tone match the client's defined tone pillars in `Brand/Brand_Voice_Identity.md`?
- [ ] Is the language and dialect compliant (Modern Standard Arabic / Egyptian / Gulf / English)?
- [ ] Is the level of technical depth appropriate for the specified persona?

---

## 2. Anti-Slop Check
- [ ] Has the text been scanned against the 35 banned AI terms in `Skills/Anti_AI_Slop.md`?
- [ ] Are sentence lengths varied and rhythmic?
- [ ] Does the introduction start immediately in media res without generic throat-clearing?

---

## 3. Custom Directives Check
- [ ] Are any client-specific taboo words avoided?
- [ ] Is the designated {{PRIMARY_CTA}} correctly integrated at the conclusion?
- [ ] Are competitor mention rules respected?
